# ANProto Fame

A small research ledger for work published through Wiredove and Secure Scuttlebutt.

We study how public work leads to repeat recognition, relationships and opportunities, and how much effort that takes. Each entry connects an existing post, project or collaboration to observed interactions, later outcomes and time spent.

## First experiment

Choose a few published works and follow them for 90 days. Record meaningful interactions as they happen, review the same cohort at days 30 and 90, and keep a weekly account of publishing and follow-up effort.

Use [LEDGER.md](LEDGER.md) to start. [RESEARCH.md](RESEARCH.md) defines the measurements, [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md) defines source references and storage, and [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) gives the steps for completing the experiment.

Wiredove and SSB provide the published records. ANProto authenticates Wiredove contributions. Fame keeps source identifiers and local research notes. A published Wave collaboration can enter the ledger through its signed record or published reference.

## Status

The repository contains the research method and a ledger template. The first milestone is a completed 90-day observation with an export that preserves every source reference.

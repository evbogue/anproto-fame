## Acceptance and handoff

A usable first entry contains a real work reference, a question, dates/status, an evidence-backed observation or an honest “no observations yet,” and effort with its measurement limits. Empty templates are preparation, not a completed experiment. The selected cohort and review dates must be explicit when observation begins.

Use manual notes and existing analytics first. Missing visibility, conversations elsewhere, selection bias, and uncertain attribution belong in the record. Page views, raw follower totals, and automated output volume alone do not establish fame or sustainability.

At each scheduled review, preserve a dated export, verify its references independently, and recommend one next product/practice change supported by the observations. A 90-day review cannot be completed early by filling in a template.

**Stop point for the next session:** one prepared or genuinely started observation and a manageable weekly recording routine. Do not build new tracking infrastructure, collect invasive identity data, or launch an automated publishing campaign.

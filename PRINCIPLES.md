# Principles

## 1. Own the canonical version

Important work should have a durable home under infrastructure the creator controls.

Platforms are distribution channels, not the source of truth.

## 2. Optimize for durable recognition

Reach matters, but recognition that survives time, platform changes, and context changes matters more.

We care about whether people remember who made something, why it mattered, and how to find the creator again.

## 3. Relationships beat raw audience size

A thousand passive followers can be less useful than ten people who repeatedly collaborate, invite, cite, recommend, or introduce.

The system should make those relationships legible.

## 4. Portability is a product requirement

A creator should be able to leave a platform without abandoning identity, archive, relationships, or reputation.

Export is not a backup feature. It is core architecture.

## 5. Provenance should travel with content

Posts, media, remixes, quotations, and derivatives should preserve a verifiable path back to their origin when practical.

ANProto can help here, but provenance must remain useful outside ANProto-aware systems.

## 6. Minimize maintenance burden

A system for sustainable fame fails if it requires permanent posting labor.

We explicitly measure the time and attention needed to stay visible.

## 7. Automate distribution, not identity

AI can adapt, summarize, clip, schedule, and syndicate.

It should not erase the creator's voice or manufacture an artificial persona.

## 8. No universal reputation score

Reputation is contextual.

A useful system records evidence: collaborations, endorsements, citations, invitations, projects, talks, repeat engagement, and outcomes.

Different communities can interpret that evidence differently.

## 9. Online fame should connect to real life

The project should track outcomes beyond impressions:

- conversations
- introductions
- invitations
- collaborations
- jobs and contracts
- event attendance
- press
- projects
- friendships and community

## 10. Protocols serve the research

ANProto, SSB, Git, HTTP, RSS, ActivityPub, ATProto, Nostr, or future systems are implementation choices.

We should use whichever combination makes sustainable fame easier to study and practice.
